Here’s my balcony. Which herbs would actually grow there? It gets sun until about 2 pm.
Assistant

Morning sun and a sheltered corner is a good start. From the photo, the railing side gets about five hours of direct light, which suits most Mediterranean herbs.

  1. Thyme and oregano like the dry heat near the railing.
  2. Chives are happy in the half shade by the door.
  3. Mint grows well too; give it its own pot, because it spreads.

Basil is worth a try after May, in the sunniest spot. Water in the morning and let the soil dry between waterings.

Which of those can I still sow in November?
Assistant

Only chives, and only indoors on a bright windowsill. Thyme, oregano and mint are easier bought as small plants in spring, and basil needs warmth, so wait until May.

Orb and composer states

Design specimen, not a screen of the product. Each composer is the phone layout (+, keyboard, orb). Levels are simulated here; in the app they come from the microphone and from read-aloud playback.

Switch states to watch the one contour morph: sphere, lit rounded square with the Stop symbol, raised hand. 650 ms, cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1).

1 · Idle

Breathes slowly (±1.8%, 7.4 s), ribbons turn once every 40 s. Label Tap to speak.

2 · Listening

Listening

Ribbons swell and twist with the microphone level (WebAudio AnalyserNode RMS). Three seconds of silence prepares the text for review.

3 · Review before sending

The transcription lands in the message box, below any draft. Nothing is sent until Send, unless Send voice messages automatically is on.

4 · Working

The sphere becomes a lit rounded square with a mint/lavender Stop symbol; light pulses every 3.5 s. Label Stop assistant.

5 · Needs you

Needs you

The cords become an orange raised hand. A tap opens Approve, Deny and Approve all; it never approves by itself.

6 · Reading aloud

Playback level (an AnalyserNode on the audio element) moves the ribbons; the answer’s own speaker shows pause and stop.

7 · Attachment waiting to send

balcony.jpg

Files you add sit above the composer as small chips until you send or remove them. Sent attachments show as small thumbnails in your message.

8 · Latest messages

Appears when you scroll up while a reply is arriving; a tap returns to the reply and resumes following it.
